ABOUT ZEBRA & FAQ

Zebra is a free daily healthcare trivia game for medical, nursing, pharmacy, dental, PA and NP students and professionals.

What is Zebra?

Zebra is a free daily trivia game for healthcare students and professionals. Every day you get 7 prompts, like "Name a cause of chest pain" or "Name an antibiotic." Correct but obvious answers barely score; rare correct answers send your zebra diving deep. The name comes from the medical saying "when you hear hoofbeats, think horses, not zebras."

How do you play?

Pick your field, read the prompt and type one answer within 20 seconds. Wrong answers don't end the round, so you can keep guessing or pass. Small typos get a "Did you mean…?" prompt. Each point is 10 µm of depth, and a perfect game reaches 7,000 µm.

Which fields are included?

Medicine (for physicians, PAs and NPs), Nursing, Pharmacy and Dental each have their own daily prompts, and Mixed blends them all. Each field gets a new set of 7 prompts every midnight, the same for everyone in that field.

Is there board exam practice?

Yes. Board Prep mode has exam-style prompts for USMLE and COMLEX, the PANCE, NP boards (AANP and ANCC), the NCLEX, the NAPLEX and dental boards.

How are scores ranked?

After each daily dive you see a bell curve and a percentile that compare you only with people in your own profession and mode, plus a career title from student to the top of your field. Daily streaks track how many days in a row you've played.

Is Zebra free?

Yes. Zebra is free, needs no account and works in any browser on phone or computer.
